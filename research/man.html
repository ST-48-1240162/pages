Mountaineering Acoustics Network

The Mountaineering Acoustics Network (MAN) is a non-profit organization established in 2025, and it is a part of the Mountaineering Acoustics Network & Terrain Lab Equipment (MANTLE). We organize field recording sessions "soundex", which are designed to promote the idea of mountaineering acoustics.
